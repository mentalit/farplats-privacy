Farplats — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Farplats ("the App") is developed by Denis ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All data you create in the App — houses, rooms, walls, measurements, and assigned articles — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Camera Access

The App uses your device's camera solely for augmented reality (AR) wall measurement. Camera data is processed in real time on your device by Apple's ARKit framework. No images, video, or AR data are recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Third-Party Advertising

The free version of the App displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use certain data for advertising purposes, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Identifier for Advertisers, or IDFA)
  • General interaction data (such as ad impressions and taps)
  • IP address (used by Google for approximate geographic targeting)

This data is collected by Google, not by us. We do not have access to any personally identifiable information collected by AdMob.

Before any tracking occurs, the App requests your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you decline tracking, AdMob will show non-personalized ads and will not access your IDFA.

If you purchase the Plus or Pro upgrade, all advertisements are removed and AdMob no longer collects any data.

Google's privacy policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional one-time (non-consumable) in-app purchases to unlock additional features and remove ads. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or have access to your payment information.

Web Browsing

The App includes a built-in web browser for browsing furniture articles. When you use this browser, your browsing activity is handled directly between your device and the websites you visit. We do not track, log, or store your browsing history.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App uses third-party advertising in the free version and includes unrestricted web access, it carries a 12+ age rating on the App Store.

Data Retention

Since all App data is stored locally on your device, you control it entirely. Deleting the App removes all associated data. We retain no data on any server.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
